Are you ready to hire?

    
Chapter 15 gives three signals. Hiring before them creates cost the business cannot carry; hiring after them creates a ceiling on income. Any one signal is a reason to look; two is a reason to act.

Your current monthly income from this business, in whole dollars.

      

      

        Signal 2 — Admin hours
        
        
Hours per month on tasks that do not require your specific expertise.

      

      

        Signal 3 — Capacity
        
        
Every declined client is lost revenue that a VA's freed capacity could recover.

    The Three-Number Value Pricing System
    
        
    Chapter 15, page 224. Value-based pricing is not a slogan — it is three numbers, and you need all three before you quote anything.
    

        
    
          Number 1 — Outcome value per month
          
          
    What the result is worth to the client each month. 8 recovered appointments × $150 = $1,200. 3 new clients from Instagram × $200 = $600. Use their numbers, not yours.
    
        
    

        
    
          Your share of it
          
          
    10% of the outcome value
    
        
    

        
    
          Number 2 — Market rate range
          
    
            
            to
            
          
    
          
    Look at five comparable services on Upwork, Clutch, or by direct enquiry. Budget end to premium end. Your rate belongs in the top third.

    Service agreement — the six clauses
    
      
    Appendix G. A written agreement before any service engagement prevents the most common sources of client conflict. Tick each clause once your template covers it.
    
      
    
      
    
        Not legal advice. The book's own disclaimer applies here: this is general educational information. Laws vary by country, state and circumstance. Free templates exist at LawDepot or Rocket Lawyer, or by searching "[your country] freelance service agreement template" — but for engagements over $2,000, or with complex scope, a lawyer's review is worth the money.
