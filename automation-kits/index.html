    The ROI calculation
    Ch. 9, pp. 123–124

        Mindset Shift #9 — you are a Time Recovery Specialist
        Ch. 9, pp. 129–130
        
        
        Business owners do not want automation. They want more time, fewer dropped leads,
        lower no-show rates, and less administrative chaos. Automation is how you deliver those outcomes;
        it is not the outcome itself.
        
        
        
          
        STOP positioning
          yourself as someone who builds automation or "does tech stuff". Technical positioning attracts
          scepticism from non-technical owners and price comparison from other tech vendors.
        
          
        START positioning yourself
          as a Time Recovery Specialist: "I help local businesses recover 5–10 hours
          of staff time per week by eliminating the manual follow-up work that nobody has time to do."
