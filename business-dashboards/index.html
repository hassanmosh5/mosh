Dashboard

Computed from the figures on the Data tab. Nothing here is a forecast.

The figures

One row per period, one column per figure. These are the only numbers you have to find — every metric on the dashboard is arithmetic on this table. Leave a cell blank if you genuinely don't have it; blank is honest, zero is a claim.

Metric library

Every metric this dashboard can produce: what it means, the formula, what a sane range looks like, and the way it usually misleads people. Metrics your model doesn't support are marked.

Where each number comes from

A dashboard dies when filling it in takes an hour. For each figure your model needs: where to read it, how long it takes, and the definition trap that makes two tools disagree about the same week.

Rebuild it in a spreadsheet

This app is a template, not a home for your accounts. Here is the same dashboard as a sheet you own: the exact column layout, a working formula for every computed column with real cell references, your data as CSV, and the conditional formatting that colours it.

Review rhythm

Numbers you look at without deciding anything are decoration. This is what to look at daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly — and the decision each one is supposed to trigger.

Diagnostics

Patterns that show up across two or more metrics at once, what usually causes each, and what to check first. The ones your own figures currently match are flagged and sorted to the top.

The whole thing as a document

Everything above as one Markdown file: the model, the figures, the metric definitions with their formulas, the sources, the spreadsheet layout and the review rhythm. Hand it to a bookkeeper, a VA, or yourself in six months.

Everything stays on this device. Figures are saved to this browser's local storage as you type. Nothing is uploaded, and the file makes no network requests of any kind.

The metrics are arithmetic, not advice. Every value on the dashboard is computed from figures you entered. Default targets are conventional ranges collected from common practice — treat them as a starting line to argue with, not a benchmark your business is failing. Nothing here forecasts revenue, and none of it is accounting, tax or investment advice.
