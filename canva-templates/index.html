      Delivering a Canva template pack
      
    
      What the buyer actually receives, and why the instructions page matters more
      than it looks.
      
    
        
    
        
      Instant usability is Chapter 7's third criterion: could a buyer open this and use it
      productively within ten minutes? For a Canva pack that means a one-page PDF with the template
      link, a two-line "how to edit" instruction, the font names, and the hex codes. It is fifteen
      minutes of work and it is the difference between a five-star review and a refund request.

        About this app
        
    
        Everything here is drawn from The AI Income Blueprint — Chapter 7 (Income Stream #2:
      Selling AI-Generated Digital Products) for the product types, validation method, pricing,
      platforms, SEO and catalogue strategy; Chapter 4 for the Canva AI tool profile and the Bulk
      Create technique; and Chapter 5 for the CLEAR-formula design-brief prompt.
        
    
        This file is entirely self-contained. It makes no network requests,
      loads no fonts, scripts, images or trackers, and sends nothing anywhere. Your saved packs live
      in this browser's localStorage and nowhere else.
