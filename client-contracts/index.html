Price it on the outcome, not the hours

Chapter 15's three-number method. Number 3 is the only one you quote — the other two exist to stop you quoting your own comfort level.

Number 1 — what the outcome is worth to them, per month

Your share of that value (%)

5–15% is the band the book uses. Below 5% you are a rounding error to them and a loss to you; above 20% the conversation becomes about your fee rather than their result.

Number 2 — the market range you have actually seen

Write the five paragraphs that matter

Everything else assembles itself. These are the parts only you can write, because they came out of the client's mouth on the call.

1 — The situation, in their words

If they cannot recognise their own situation in this paragraph, nothing after it lands. Quote the sentence they actually said.

2 — Three outcomes (not tasks)

"16 posts a month" is a task. "A month of visibility your team never has to think about" is an outcome. Tasks go in the deliverables list; outcomes go here.

3 — How it works (one line per stage)
4 — Why you
Proof — one result, with a number in it

One specific, verifiable result beats three adjectives. No result yet? Delete the line rather than inventing one — the agreement you are about to sign has a warranty clause in it.

5 — The single next step

One action, one date. Two options at the end of a proposal is one more than converts.

The proposal

Not legal advice — and it matters here more than anywhere else in this repo

This is a drafting aid built from general practice, not a lawyer, and not a substitute for one. Contract law varies by country, by state and by the specific facts of your engagement. Read every clause before you send it, delete what does not apply, and for anything over a couple of thousand — or with unusual risk, personal data at scale, or a client much larger than you — have it reviewed. The book's own Appendix G says the same thing.

About this app

The book stops one step short of the documents. Chapter 15 tells you to send a one-page proposal within 24 hours and to have a signed agreement before any engagement; Appendix G lists the clauses that agreement needs and points you at a template site. This app is that missing step — it writes both documents, from your numbers, in a form you can send.

Clause bodies, pricing method and follow-up cadence are drawn from The AI Income Blueprint by Hassan Mohammed (principally Chapter 15 and Appendix G, with service-specific terms from Chapters 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12). The legal mechanics — statutory interest, data protection, contractor status — are general practice, not book content, and are flagged as such where they appear.

This file is entirely self-contained. It makes no network requests, loads no fonts, scripts, images or trackers, and sends nothing anywhere. Your engagement lives in this browser's localStorage and nowhere else. ⬇ Back up downloads the lot as JSON; 🗑 Clear backs up first, then wipes.
