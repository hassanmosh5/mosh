    Design backwards from the proof

    Start at the outcome and ask what someone must be able to do to reach it. Those are your modules. Then ask what they must be able to do to finish each module. Those are your lessons. Anything that does not sit on that path is a bonus, not a lesson — mark it as such and it stops inflating your build.
