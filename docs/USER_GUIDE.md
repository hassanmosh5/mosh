# User guide

MOSH Chief of Staff lives at `/cos`. Sign in, and the first account to arrive
becomes the workspace owner.

## The daily loop

**Morning.** Open the dashboard. *Today's priorities* is the ranked list — not
sorted by date, but by the founder prioritisation model:

1. Critical client issues
2. Revenue-generating activity
3. High-value strategic work
4. Deadlines
5. Business risks
6. Important operational work
7. Automation opportunities
8. Administrative work

Each task carries a label — **URGENT**, **HIGH VALUE**, **DELEGATABLE**,
**AUTOMATABLE**, **LOW VALUE** — and hovering the score shows why it ranked
where it did.

Press **Scan for alerts** to recompute notifications, or **Morning briefing** to
generate the full executive briefing.

**During the day.** Work from Tasks. Statuses change inline from the list. The
command centre (**⌘K** / **Ctrl+K**) jumps to anything: type a client name, a
project, a task, or a command like "Create project" or "Generate briefing".

**Weekly.** Generate the Weekly Executive Report in Reports. Review Goals for
anything marked *Behind pace*, and Automations for the highest-scoring
candidates.

## Sections

**Dashboard** — the snapshot: overdue work, project health, weighted pipeline,
month-to-date profit, priorities, alerts, goals, automation candidates.

**AI Chief of Staff** — ask for a plan, a review or an analysis. It reads your
live data through controlled tools before answering. Try:

- *What's the most important thing I should do today?*
- *Review my projects and tell me which one is slipping.*
- *What's blocking revenue?*
- *Which clients need attention?*
- *What can I delegate, and what can I automate?*
- *Turn this idea into a 90-day plan and create the tasks.*

**Tasks** — statuses run Backlog → Planned → In Progress → Blocked → Review →
Completed (or Cancelled); priorities are Critical, High, Medium, Low. Tasks can
carry a project, client, goal, tags, estimated and actual effort, dependencies
and a recurrence rule. Completing a recurring task creates the next occurrence
automatically.

**Projects** — health is computed, not chosen: **GREEN** / **YELLOW** / **RED**
from deadline pressure against completion, overdue and blocked tasks, open risks
and budget burn. A missed deadline with work outstanding is always red. The
health note on each project says exactly which signals fired.

**CRM** — pipeline stages run New Lead → Qualified → Contacted → Proposal →
Negotiation → Won/Lost. *Weighted pipeline* multiplies each deal by its
probability, which is the number worth forecasting on. Set a follow-up date on
every live lead — stale leads generate alerts.

**Goals** — annual through daily, with a KPI, target and current value. Progress
is computed, and a goal whose progress lags the elapsed time by more than 15
points is flagged **Behind pace**. Link tasks to goals so the prioritiser knows
which work moves the number.

**Content** — the calendar across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok,
LinkedIn, blog and email. Statuses run Idea → Research → Script → Production →
Review → Scheduled → Published → Repurpose.

**Products** — digital products through Idea → Research → Validation →
Production → Launch → Optimisation → Scale, with price, units and revenue.

**Knowledge** — services, pricing, SOPs, brand guidelines, policies. The AI
searches this before answering anything about how MOSH works, so what you put
here directly improves its answers. **Memory** below it holds structured facts
with a source, a confidence score and an optional expiry; reusing a key updates
the fact rather than duplicating it.

**AI Agents** — thirteen specialists. Enter an objective and leave the agent on
*Route automatically*: the orchestrator picks the specialists, runs them, and the
Chief of Staff combines their output into one recommendation. Each agent's
prompt and tool allow-list is editable.

**Automations** — log a repetitive process and the system scores it out of 100
from frequency, time saved, complexity and business value. *Detected candidates*
below are found automatically from your own recurring and repeated tasks.

**Analytics** — revenue, expenses, profit and margin by month; pipeline value by
stage; product revenue; delivery throughput; AI usage and estimated cost.

**Reports** — Daily Briefing, Weekly Executive, Monthly Business, Quarterly
Strategy. Each runs Performance → Problems → Opportunities → Risks →
Recommendations → Next actions, assembled from your records and then interpreted
by the AI.

**Settings** — business details, business areas, people and roles, approvals,
integrations and the audit log.

## Approvals

The AI will not send a client email, publish content, change a price, commit
money or delete data. When it wants one of those, it produces a plan and the
request appears in **Settings → Approvals**. You approve or reject it, carry out
the action, then mark it executed — which puts your name against it in the audit
trail. Requests expire after seven days.

## Reading the numbers

- **REPORTED** — what you or an integration entered.
- **CALCULATED** — derived from reported data (profit, margin, weighted
  pipeline, completion rates, health scores).
- **AI ESTIMATE** — produced by the AI and stored separately; never mixed into
  the calculated totals.

The financial module is an operational summary. It is not accounting or tax
advice.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including settings, finance writes, approvals and the audit log |
| Manager | Run operations: projects, CRM, goals, products, automations; read finance |
| Member | Read most things; write tasks, content and knowledge |
| Viewer | Read only |
| Client | Reserved for a future client portal |

New accounts join as **Viewer**. Promote them in Settings → People.

## Tips

- Fill the knowledge base first. Ten good documents change the quality of every
  AI answer more than any prompt tweak.
- Record actual minutes on tasks. That is what makes automation scoring and the
  detected candidates accurate.
- Set follow-up dates on every live lead — the alert scan works from them.
- Link tasks to goals. Unlinked work looks like admin to the prioritiser, because
  usually it is.
