Step-by-step solutions

The answer is not the lesson. The working is the lesson.

Most students already know what the answer was. What they can’t do is get there under pressure. Every question in ExamAce opens up like this.

WASSCE Core MathsSequences & seriesAsked in 9 of the last 12 years

The 4th term of an Arithmetic Progression is 13 and the 10th term is 31. Find the sum of the first 20 terms.

  1. Write down the rule before you touch the numbers. An A.P. is fully described by its first term a and common difference d. Uₙ = a + (n − 1)d
  2. Turn each sentence into an equation. “4th term is 13” and “10th term is 31” are two equations in the same two unknowns. a + 3d = 13 …(i) a + 9d = 31 …(ii)
  3. Eliminate a by subtracting. Subtracting (i) from (ii) removes a completely. 6d = 18 ⟹ d = 3
  4. Substitute back to find a. Put d = 3 into equation (i). a + 9 = 13 ⟹ a = 4
  5. Now answer the question that was asked. The sum of the first n terms, with n = 20, a = 4, d = 3. Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] S₂₀ = 10 [8 + 57] = 10 × 65 = 650 Examiner’s note: a large number of candidates stop at d = 3 and lose four marks. The question asked for S₂₀ — write the final statement, with units where they apply.

Why this matters: in the real paper, steps 1–4 carry method marks even if the arithmetic slips. We teach the layout that earns them.

Questions

The things parents ask us first

How much data does it use?
A normal study day — one lesson, one question set, one drill — is roughly 15–25 MB if you watch the video, and under 2 MB if you read the text version instead. Lessons can be downloaded on Wi-Fi and watched later with no data at all.
Can I pay with Mobile Money?
Yes — MoMo is the main payment method. MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money are all supported. You get a prompt on your phone, approve it with your PIN, and an SMS receipt follows. Bundles can be paid in two or three instalments.
What exactly does “guaranteed” mean?
It means your money is guaranteed, not WAEC’s grading. If you complete at least 50 of the 60 daily sessions, sit all the scheduled mocks, and your grade in that subject does not improve, we refund your subscription in full to the same MoMo wallet. The conditions are listed in the guarantee section above, in full, before you pay.
Are you affiliated with WAEC?
No. ExamAce is an independent study platform. Our tutors are experienced teachers and examiners who have marked WAEC scripts, but we are not endorsed by, affiliated with, or connected to the West African Examinations Council. Anyone who tells you otherwise is not us.
My child is in JHS 3 — is this the same as the SHS material?
No. BECE and WASSCE have separate plans, separate question banks and separate mock papers. You pick the exam at sign-up and only ever see material for that exam.
What if we start late — is 60 days really enough?
The plan compresses. Tell it your exam date and it re-spaces the same content into the time you have; below about 30 days it switches to a triage mode that leads with the highest-frequency topics first. Starting late is common — the plan is built to expect it.
Can a parent see whether the work is actually being done?
Yes. Add a parent number and you get a short summary every Sunday: days completed, mock scores, the topic that improved most, and the topic still costing marks. It is sent by WhatsApp, or SMS if you prefer.
