AILaunch Kit

AI Income Launch Kit — a companion app for The AI Income Blueprint: How Non-Tech Professionals Are Earning $1,000–$5,000 Per Month With Simple AI Tools by Hassan Mohammed (First Edition, 2025). Every framework, table, day plan, rate and script in this app is taken from the book; chapter references are shown throughout.

Everything you type stays in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, and the app makes no network requests — it works fully offline.

The income figures reproduced here are the book's, drawn from the experiences of real practitioners. They are not guarantees of results. Nothing here is legal, financial or professional advice.
