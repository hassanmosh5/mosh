2. Build the prompt

Every control below maps to one letter of the CLEAR Formula from Chapter 5 of The AI Income Blueprint. Choose a preset or select Write my own… in any dropdown.

R Role — the expertise the AI adopts

The single highest-leverage line in the prompt. Specific credentials beat job titles.

C Context — the situation the AI cannot know

Include the real constraint and any number you actually have. Vague context is the #1 cause of generic output.

A Audience — who reads the result

Skipping Audience is the most common CLEAR mistake — it is what makes copy land or read like a brochure.

$ Offer — the income stream you are selling

Sets which of the book's seven income streams this prompt serves, and filters the task list below.

▸ Task — what you want produced

One task per prompt. Stuffing three requests into one prompt degrades all three.

L Length — exact format and size

Without this you get default medium-length prose, which is almost never what you needed.

E Example — the tone or format to match

A pasted sample of your own writing is worth more than any adjective.

Matched takes the role, context, audience and offer from one row of the workbook, so they describe a single coherent business. Mixed recombines all four freely — far more variety, but check the combination makes sense before you sell the output.

The CLEAR Formula

    
From Chapter 5, The Art of the Prompt. A CLEAR prompt takes about 90 seconds longer to write than a weak one and saves 10–20× that in editing.

    

      

        
PartWhat it doesWhat goes wrong without it

        
          
CContextThe situation, background and specifics that frame the task.The AI has no knowledge of your situation unless you give it. Output is generic.

          
LLengthExact format, structure, word count, output type.You get default medium-length prose, which is almost never what you needed.

          
EExampleA sample of the tone or format to match.Tone drifts to the model's house style instead of yours.

          
AAudienceWho reads this, what they care about, what they respond to.The most commonly skipped part — and the reason copy reads like a brochure.

          
RRoleThe expertise the AI should adopt.You get a competent generalist instead of a specialist.

The 3-Pass Method

    
Refine the output instead of accepting the first draft.

    
    
      
  1. Draft pass — generate the initial version. Judge structure only, not wording.
    2. 
      
  2. Accuracy & depth pass — check every claim, add what only you know, cut what is invented.
    3. 
      
  3. Voice & polish pass — make it sound like a person, and specifically like you.

About this generator

    
Built on the Wealth Ideas Matrix: 10 wealth ideas, each with 10 expert roles, 10 business contexts, 10 audiences and 10 offers. Every preset in the dropdowns is a real row from that workbook — none of them are filler. Combined freely, that is 99,000 distinct prompts. 

    
The prompt structure, the 3-Pass Method, the seven income streams, the platform comparison and the price bands are all from The AI Income Blueprint by Hassan Mohammed.

    
This file is entirely self-contained. It makes no network requests, loads no fonts, scripts, images or trackers, and sends nothing anywhere. Your saved prompts live in this browser's localStorage and nowhere else.
