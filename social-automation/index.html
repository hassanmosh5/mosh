Social Media Content Automation Supply · capacity · cadence

Nothing here is a forecast. Every figure on every tab is arithmetic on numbers you typed. The app can tell you that a five-a-week cadence needs eleven hours you do not have; it cannot tell you whether anyone will watch.

The CLEAR Formula (Context, Length, Examples, Audience, Role) and the 3-Pass Method come from Chapter 5 of The AI Income Blueprint by Hassan Mohammed. Chapter 8 covers social media management as a service and Chapter 12 covers content at scale — the marketing-toolkit and youtube-toolkit apps in this repo cover those. The production model here — the three ceilings, the batching curve, the payback arithmetic, the platform specification table — is this app's own, and says so wherever it appears.

Everything you type stays in this browser's local storage. No network requests of any kind are made by this page.
